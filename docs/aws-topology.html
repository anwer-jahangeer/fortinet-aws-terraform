Routing and control plane

  • All FortiGates use iBGP AS 65000.
  • Hub1 and Hub2 act as route reflectors for branch loopbacks and LAN routes.
  • Branches advertise only their LAN-connected prefixes through route maps.
  • ADVPN initially traverses a hub and can form a dynamic direct spoke shortcut.
  • FortiManager manages device settings, SD-WAN rules, objects, and policy packages.
